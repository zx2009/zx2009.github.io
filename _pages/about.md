I am Zixuan Gao, a high school student at Tesla STEM High School with a passion for computer science and visual arts. My research interests include computer vision, generative models, and reinforcement learning.

Outside of research, I am a competitive Go player, visual artist, pianist, and violinist. I look forward to exploring the intersections of technology, creativity, and human expression.
