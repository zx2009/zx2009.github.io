# Selected Publications & Reports

## Peer-Reviewed Publications

### RubricRL: Simple Generalizable Rewards for Text-to-Image Generation

Xuelu Feng, Yunsheng Li, Ziyu Wan, Zixuan Gao, Junsong Yuan, Dongdong Chen, Chunming Qiao

*European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV), 2026*

RubricRL introduces a reinforcement learning framework that replaces black-box reward signals with prompt-specific rubrics. A multimodal judge independently evaluates fine-grained criteria such as object correctness, attribute accuracy, text rendering, and visual realism, while adaptive weighting prioritizes criteria relevant to each prompt. 

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20651)

## Research Reports

### Improving Old Portrait Restoration with Rubric-Guided Reinforcement Learning

**Zixuan Gao**

*Independent Research, 2026*

This study investigates whether restoration-specific reinforcement learning can improve old portrait restoration while preserving facial identity and appearance. Using 2,301 training images and a ten-criterion rubric reward, the adapted Qwen-Image-Edit model achieved a 48.0% increase in mean rubric score and was preferred in 73.3% of 1,100 human evaluations, compared to 8.8% for the baseline.
